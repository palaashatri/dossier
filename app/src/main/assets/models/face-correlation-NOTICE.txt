Dossier optional strong face-correlation model pack
===================================================

The Android application does not bundle these model binaries. After explicit
user consent it can download pinned, checksum-verified copies from the official
OpenCV Model Zoo repository.

-------
OpenCV Android AAR: org.opencv:opencv:4.13.0
OpenCV license: Apache License 2.0

Dossier uses YuNet only to detect a face and its five landmarks, then uses
OpenCV FaceRecognizerSF.alignCrop and SFace to compute an on-device embedding.
No selected image, aligned crop, landmark, or embedding is sent to Dossier or a
third-party service. Results are supporting evidence and never proof of identity.
